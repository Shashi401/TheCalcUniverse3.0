How is a monthly mortgage payment calculated?

A monthly mortgage payment is calculated with the amortization formula, where P is the loan amount, r the monthly interest rate, and n the number of monthly payments. For a $320,000 loan at 6.8% over 30 years, that is $2,086.16 per month before taxes and insurance.

M = P × [ r(1+r)ⁿ ] / [ (1+r)ⁿ − 1 ]

Frequently asked questions

Why is my bank’s payment higher than this calculator’s?

Lender quotes typically include escrow — property taxes, homeowners insurance, and mortgage insurance (PMI) — collected on top of principal and interest. Enter those amounts above and the totals will match your lender’s estimate.

What is the 28/36 rule?

The 28/36 rule is a lender guideline: spend at most 28% of gross monthly income on housing and at most 36% on all debt combined. Enter your income above and this page checks your payment against it automatically.
